But who am I?

About Me

Hi, I’m Nicolas an Email Designer focused on creating high-performing, visually effective email experiences.

I specialize in translating marketing campaigns into email layouts that are clear, engaging, and built to perform. I pay close attention to hierarchy, spacing, and content flow to guide user behavior and improve click-through rates, while ensuring consistency across devices and email clients.

My approach combines design thinking with a strong understanding of email constraints. I design with real-world limitations in mind from mobile responsiveness and dark mode to client-specific rendering making sure every decision supports both usability and performance.

I also work with modular systems and reusable components, helping scale email production without sacrificing quality. Whether refining existing templates or building new ones, I focus on improving structure, clarity, and overall campaign effectiveness.

At the core, I aim to design emails that not only look good, but communicate better, perform consistently, and drive results.

Improving Mobile Experience & Visual Messaging in Email Design.

Redesigned a promotional email layout to improve mobile usability and enhance message clarity through better visual hierarchy and content structure.

The goal was to simplify the layout, improve readability, and highlight key value propositions more effectively through visual and structural adjustments.

  • Optimized layout for mobile readability and spacing.
  • Improved visual hierarchy to guide user attention.
  • Reorganized content for better scanning and clarity.
  • Refined CTA placement for increased visibility.
  • Introduced animated hero to highlight key messages.

Focused on reducing cognitive load and making the main message immediately clear, especially in mobile environments where space and attention are limited.
